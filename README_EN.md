# Chong-Zhen ChatGPT Top-up Guide

[中文](README.md) | English

A plain guide to [Chong-Zhen](https://www.chong-zhen.com/), a ChatGPT subscription top-up service: how ordering works, which plan fits, how payment and invoices work, and where to get help. Everything here is taken from the public pages of the site. Prices and usage limits change, so check the site and your own ChatGPT account for current figures.

## What it is

Chong-Zhen is a ChatGPT subscription top-up service operated by Hong Kong Prompt Limited. You choose Plus, Pro 10x (previously called 5x) or Pro 20x, pay with Alipay, and the plan is activated on your own ChatGPT account, usually within 1-3 minutes. You continue using your existing ChatGPT account.

The site describes itself as an independent third-party service. It is not an OpenAI website and has no partnership with OpenAI.

## Who it suits

- Users without a USD card. Prices are shown in RMB for reference and settled in US dollars through Alipay at the day's exchange rate.
- People whose card was declined on OpenAI's own checkout page.
- Individuals and teams that need an invoice, or companies that prefer a bank transfer.
- Developers who use Codex. Codex has no separate top-up; it draws on the ChatGPT plan of the account you sign in with.

## Before you order

The [Plus page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/plus) lists five things to check:

1. The account is on the free tier with no active plan. Plans cannot be stacked.
2. The account is your own.
3. The account was not registered with an Outlook or Hotmail address.
4. You have decided whether you need an invoice. It can only be chosen when ordering.
5. You have a phone number and Alipay ready.

## How ordering works

1. Pick a plan on the [home page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/).
2. Enter a phone number. It is used to match your order and guarantee period, and is unrelated to the ChatGPT account being topped up.
3. Choose whether you need a company invoice and accept the terms.
4. Pay with Alipay: scan the QR code on desktop, or jump to the app on mobile.
5. You receive an activation code with instructions. Follow the tutorial on the order page to finish activation.
6. Refresh ChatGPT. When the plan name appears in Settings, the top-up has arrived.

A step-by-step version with screenshots is on the [payment methods and tutorial page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/chongzhi-fangshi).

## Choosing a plan

| Plan | Who the site says it is for | Activation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plus | Everyday writing, spreadsheets and slides, occasional coding | 1-3 minutes after payment |
| Pro 10x (was 5x) | Daily Codex use, when Plus limits run out often | 1-3 minutes after payment |
| Pro 20x | Heavy development, large projects, long-running tasks | 1-3 minutes after payment |

A higher Pro 50x plan is ordered from the [Pro page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/chatgpt-pro) and is activated manually by support after payment.

The site does not promise a fixed multiplier or message count. Actual limits are whatever the Usage page of your account shows after activation. If you are unsure, start with one month of Plus, watch how fast the limit runs out, then decide. There is a [plan comparison page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/plus/taocan-duibi) on the site.

## Pricing rules

No figures are listed here on purpose. See the [price page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/chatgpt-pro/jiage); the amount on the payment page is final.

- Orders are priced in US dollars. The RMB number is a converted reference, so the amount Alipay charges can differ slightly.
- Order amount = OpenAI's price + a service fee. The fee is capped, and the cap is stated in the [terms of service](https://www.chong-zhen.com/terms). The site states there are no other charges.
- All plans are monthly. There is no quarterly or yearly billing.
- Orders with an invoice are settled at a slightly higher invoice price.

## Invoices and company payment

- An invoice must be selected when ordering. The page shows the amount with invoice before you pay.
- If you choose "no invoice", the order cannot be changed later and an invoice cannot be issued afterwards.
- After payment, you request the invoice yourself on the order page and receive it by email.
- Larger company orders can be paid by bank transfer, and companies can sign a long-term contract. Contact support to confirm and arrange it.

Details are in the site's [invoice guide](https://www.chong-zhen.com/jiaocheng/fapiao).

## FAQ

**Whose account gets the plan?**
Yours. Chat history and custom settings stay as they are.

**How long does it take?**
Plus, Pro 10x and Pro 20x usually arrive within 1-3 minutes of payment. Pro 50x is activated manually by support.

**Can I add a month on top of an active plan?**
No. Wait until the current plan expires and the account is back on the free tier.

**What does the guarantee cover?**
Each order has a 30-day guarantee. Interruptions not caused by the user are handled under the terms of service. Prohibited use, such as reverse proxying or sharing one account among several people, is not covered.

**Can I get a refund?**
According to the site, you can request a full refund before the activation code takes effect. After the plan is active, the 30-day guarantee applies. The terms of service are the reference.

**What should I avoid?**
The site lists five things: no reverse proxying, reverse engineering or account pools; do not extract an API key; do not ask reverse-engineering questions or run sensitive-word tests in Codex; do not use a Microsoft email address; do not share the account.

**How do I use the plan in Codex?**
Sign in to Codex with the ChatGPT account that has the plan. Do not sign in with an API key, which is billed separately. See the [Codex page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/codex).

**My card was declined elsewhere. Does that matter?**
No. A previous decline does not affect ordering here, as long as the account has no active plan.

## Orders and support

- Email support@chong-zhen.com or use the [contact page](https://www.chong-zhen.com/contact). Include your order number.
